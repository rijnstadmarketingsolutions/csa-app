Every move, explained

A price chart shows that something is moving. Vantis finds out why, and says so honestly when it can't be told yet.

What is happening now?

Vantis reads news from eleven sources, the economic calendar and the prices of hundreds of coins and stocks. Every event gets an Impact Score from 0 to 100: how big its influence on the market could be, calculated with a fixed method you can look up.

Why is it moving?

When a price makes a notable move, Vantis checks eight possible causes against data and sources: news, macro figures, trading volume, the whole market and technical levels. If a cause can't be shown, it says the cause is still unknown.

What does it mean for me?

Your overview shows at a glance how the market is doing and what is happening today. Follow your own coins and stocks, set price alerts and get a short briefing every morning at 08:00.

            FAQ

            I've never invested. Is Vantis for me?

            Yes. The Vantis Academy starts with the basics: what a stock or a blockchain is, how to trade safely and what it costs. Then you learn to read charts, make a trading plan and limit your risk, at your own pace.

            Can I actually trade with Vantis?

            Yes. You buy and sell crypto and stocks directly in Vantis, with market, limit, stop and trailing orders and a take-profit and stop-loss on your order.

            Does Vantis give investment advice?

            No. Vantis provides market information, analysis and education, calculated from public data with a fixed method. What you do with it is up to you. Investing involves risk; you can lose some or all of your money.

            Where does the data come from?

            Prices come from sources including Bitvavo and Kraken, market figures from CoinGecko and CoinPaprika, and news from sources such as CoinDesk, Cointelegraph, CNBC and the Financial Times. Every insight shows its source and time.

            How is my account secured?

            You always log in with your password and a code from an authenticator app, such as Google or Microsoft Authenticator. Your data is stored encrypted in the EU.

            Does Vantis work on my phone?

            Yes. Open Vantis in your browser and add it to your home screen; then you get push notifications too. Apps in the App Store and Play Store will follow later.

            What does it cost?

            An account is free. The Academy comes with Plus (€4.99 per month), certificates with Pro (€12.99), live guidance with Elite (€29.99) and 1-on-1 coaching with Ultimate (€59.99). You can try every plan free for a week and cancel any time after that.
